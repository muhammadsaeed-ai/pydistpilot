# Community code of conduct

PyDistPilot aims to maintain a respectful, constructive, and inclusive community.

Participants are expected to be courteous, assume good intent while being precise about technical disagreements, welcome questions from newcomers, and focus criticism on code, behavior, and evidence rather than people. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, and publication of private information without consent are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through GitHub's repository contact or reporting tools. Do not publish another person's private details in an issue.
